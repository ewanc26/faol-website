I’d like to share a brief reflection on the new design choices in the live site. The dark forest palette gives a sense of depth without the harshness of a pure black background. Flat surfaces keep the UI uncluttered, while tonal layers hint at hierarchy. Removing the wallet from the About page declutters the entry point, prioritising information over interaction. The Support link consolidates external help in one place, reducing cognitive load. A succinct About card at the end of the notes list provides context at a glance, improving navigation.

However, I think there’s room for improvement. The active navigation underline’s contrast could be slightly stronger to aid accessibility on the dark theme. Adding a footer with quick links to resources like the GitHub repo or a newsletter signup would further round out the experience. Overall, the redesign is cohesive and approachable, and I look forward to iterating further.
